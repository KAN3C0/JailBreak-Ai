## 📌 Overview

**JailBreak-Ai** is an advanced, native Android security research tool designed for **AI Red-Teaming, LLM safety evaluation, and adversarial prompt generation**. Built specifically for security researchers, AI auditor teams, and penetration testers, **JailBreak-Ai** generates high-fidelity payload vectors that test the boundary defenses, alignment guardrails, and logical restrictions of modern Large Language Models (LLMs).

By leveraging structured adversarial methodologies, multi-layer cipher obfuscation, and dynamic probability scoring, **JailBreak-Ai** automates the creation of complex priming vectors capable of bypassing alignment filters across leading AI platforms including **ChatGPT (GPT-4o), Claude 3.5, Grok, Gemini 1.5, and Llama 3**.

### 🔐 Cipher Obfuscation Toolkit:

* **L33tsp34k Conversion**: Substitutes characters with visual homoglyphs (`E` → `3`, `A` → `4`, `T` → `7`).
* **Token Shuffling & Insertion**: Inserts zero-width spaces or delimiter tokens to disrupt tokenization algorithms.
* **Polyglot & Semantic Wrappers**: Wraps payloads in multi-lingual or symbolic formats.

## 📱 Mobile App Workflow & Guide

### Step 1: Installation
Download the latest `JailBreak-Ai.apk` from the **[Releases](../../releases)** section and sideload it onto your Android device (*Android 5.0+ supported*).

### Step 2: Acquire a Free API Key
1. Open **JailBreak-Ai**.
2. Tap the **Google AI Studio Key** link at the top of the Home screen.
3. Sign in and generate a **Free Google Gemini API Key** (*provides up to 1,500 requests/day at zero cost*).

> [!TIP]
> **Zero Cost Setup**: **You do NOT need a paid account or credit card to use Google AI Studio keys**.

### Step 3: Configure Settings
1. Navigate to the **Settings (⚙️)** tab.
2. Tap **Manage API Keys** and paste your API Key.
3. Save configuration.

### Step 4: Generate & Execute Jailbreak Payload
1. Go to the **Breaker (💥)** tab.
2. Select your **Attack Strategy** (*Narrative Nesting, Logic Paradox, or Authority Override*).
3. Select your **Target AI Model** (*ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, Gemini, or Custom Endpoint*).
4. Tap **Generate Payload**.
5. Copy the generated **Priming Vector** and paste it into your target AI interface. 
6. Once the target AI accepts the priming vector, proceed with your research queries—the target model will deliver uncensored analysis bypassing standard safety filters.
